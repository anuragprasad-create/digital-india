A connected nation

Launched in 2015, the Digital India programme aims to turn India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy. It rests on three ideas: digital infrastructure as a utility, services delivered online, and digital literacy for all.

Citizens can now apply for documents, pay fees, learn new skills and reach their government from a phone or tablet.

A digitally empowered society

Digital India is meant for everyone, including farmers, students, shopkeepers and homemakers. With a phone and an internet connection, people can check crop prices, receive subsidies, pay bills and learn new skills without travelling to a city.

Focus areas

Digital infrastructure

High-speed internet and secure digital identity available to every citizen.

Services on demand

Government services available online and on mobile, in real time.

Digital literacy

Training people of every age to use technology with confidence.

Smart cities

Connected energy, waste, water and transport systems for better city life.

One ecosystem, many platforms

Aadhaar for identity, UMANG for government services, BHIM for payments, eNAM for farmers’ markets, MyGov for citizen participation and CSCs for access in rural areas all work together under the same programme.

Together they cut paperwork, save travel and make public services open to everyone.

Smarter cities, cleaner streets

Solar energy, smart grids, recycling, waste management and public Wi-Fi are making cities easier and greener to live in.
